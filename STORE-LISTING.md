# Chrome Web Store Listing — Jobinzy

Everything needed for the store listing, in one place. The **icons** are
already generated by `scripts/gen-icons.mjs` (runs on `npm run build` and
emits `public/icons/`). **Screenshots and the developer-account steps are
human-required** (AGENT.md §5) — flagged below.

## Short description (~132 char limit)

> Autofill job applications from your saved profile — with AI drafts for
> open-ended questions, all stored privately on your device.

## Longer description (paste-ready)

Jobinzy helps you fill job application forms faster and stop retyping the
same details. Click the Jobinzy button while on any application page —
Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, Wuzzuf, or any other site — and a review
panel appears with every detected field already matched against your saved
profile.

- **Instant autofill** of name, email, phone, national ID, and links
  (LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub, Drive) — pulled straight from your profile,
  no AI needed.
- **AI drafts** for open-ended questions ("Why are you a good fit?"), built
  from your CV, your profile, and the question itself. You stay in control:
  edit, rewrite with new instructions, or skip any field.
- **File & link helper** — fields that ask for a CV, video, or portfolio
  link are flagged clearly and never auto-attached; paste a link inline
  where it fits.
- **Answer bank** — answers to recurring questions are remembered locally,
  so the next form that asks the same thing is already filled. Rewrite and
  the new version is saved for that exact question.
- **Private by design** — everything is stored locally on your device in
  `chrome.storage.local`. No backend, no tracking, no accounts. AI
  generation goes directly to the provider you configure (NVIDIA free tier
  or your own OpenAI-compatible key) and only when you ask for it.
- **You always press submit** — Jobinzy fills fields; it never submits a
  form for you.

## Icons (asset requirements — verify against dashboard at submission time)

Generated by `scripts/gen-icons.mjs` into `public/icons/`. Required sizes for
Chrome Web Store submissions (verify current requirements on the dashboard,
they can change):

- 16px, 32px, 48px, 128px (manifest `action.default_icon`)
- 128px and larger for the store tile (the store accepts a 128x128 icon;
  larger promotional tiles may be required)

## Screenshots (HUMAN-REQUIRED)

Take 2–3 screenshots showing the review panel mid-use on a real form:

1. **Detection results** — the panel listing fields with status chips
   (auto-filled / AI-generated) on a job application form.
2. **AI-generated draft** — an open-ended question with a generated answer
   and the Rewrite control visible.
3. **After Fill form** — the form's own fields visibly populated.

Recommended dimensions per current Chrome Web Store spec: 1280x800 or
640x400. Re-capture any time the UI changes.

## Developer account (HUMAN-REQUIRED)

Register at the Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard (one-time $5 fee) and
complete the trader/non-trader declaration before creating the listing. The
registration itself cannot be automated.
