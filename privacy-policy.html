Jobinzy Privacy Policy

Last updated: version 0.1.0

Jobinzy is a browser extension that helps you fill job application forms faster. This policy explains exactly what data the extension handles, where it lives, and what leaves your device.

1. What we collect

All of the following is entered by you, stored only on your device:

DataWhere it is stored
Full name, email address, phone number, national ID, addresschrome.storage.local (profile)
Links you save (portfolio, LinkedIn, GitHub, Drive, etc.)chrome.storage.local (profile)
Your CV file name and the text extracted from your CVchrome.storage.local (profile cvFileName, cvText)
Structured CV data parsed during onboarding (education, experience, skills)chrome.storage.local (profile structuredCv)
Optional onboarding Q&A answers you providechrome.storage.local (profile onboardingQA)
Your default answering style / tone instructionchrome.storage.local (profile defaultStyle)
Saved answers for recurring form questions, with optional custom rewrite instructions and the page where each was first seenchrome.storage.local (answer bank)
Your AI provider settings: provider choice, base URL, model, API keychrome.storage.local (AI settings)
Extension metadata (onboarding-complete flag, schema version)chrome.storage.local

2. Where your data is stored

Everything above is stored locally on your own device in chrome.storage.local. Jobinzy operates no backend server and has no server-side storage. Nothing you enter is uploaded to any server controlled by the extension's developer — there is no such server.

3. What is sent to third parties, and when

The only time any of your data leaves your device is when you ask Jobinzy to generate or rewrite an answer (or structure your CV), and you have configured an AI provider. At that moment, a prompt is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose and configured in Settings (NVIDIA NIM free tier, or any OpenAI-compatible provider whose key you supply). That prompt may include:

  • your CV text and structured CV data,
  • your onboarding Q&A answers and default style instruction,
  • the text of the specific form question you are answering, and
  • a previously saved answer (when you ask for a rewrite).

No data is sent to the AI provider without your explicit action — the extension never generates answers silently in the background, and it never submits any form on your behalf.

4. What the extension does not do

  • It does not run automatically on pages you visit; it only acts when you click the toolbar button.
  • It does not attach files to forms, and never submits a form for you.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or fingerprinting.
  • It does not read pages you haven't explicitly triggered it on (it uses the activeTab permission, not broad site access).

5. Deleting your data

Open the Jobinzy options page → Clear all my data to permanently delete your profile, saved answers, and AI settings from the device. Removing the extension also removes everything the extension stored.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
